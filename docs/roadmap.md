# LinkBridge Roadmap

## Milestone 1: Local Pairing

- Advertise and discover `_linkbridge._tcp.local`.
- Show discovered peers in both apps.
- Exchange public keys.
- Confirm a six-digit pairing code.
- Persist trusted peer records.

## Milestone 2: File Transfer

- Android to Mac transfer.
- Mac to Android transfer.
- Progress, cancel, and retry.
- SHA-256 verification after transfer.
- Android share sheet integration.
- Mac drag-and-drop sender.

## Milestone 3: Wireless Display, Android to Mac

- Android `MediaProjection` capture.
- Hardware H.264 encoding with `MediaCodec`.
- Mac receive/decode path.
- Orientation and resolution changes.
- Audio routing decision.

## Milestone 4: Wireless Display, Mac to Android

- macOS `ScreenCaptureKit` capture.
- H.264 encode path.
- Android receive/decode path.
- Pointer and keyboard input experiments.

## Milestone 5: Product Polish

- Auto-reconnect trusted devices.
- Transfer history.
- Clipboard sync.
- Local hotspot fallback.
- Signed releases for macOS and Android.
